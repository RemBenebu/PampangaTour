PORAC

Porac is a municipality in Pampanga known for its rich natural landscapes, including mountains, rivers, and eco-tourism destinations. It is derived from the word “purac,” referring to the abundant trees found by early settlers, and is considered one of the earliest towns established in Pampanga. It developed as an agricultural community near the Poraq River and has grown through history despite challenges such as the 1991 Mt. Pinatubo eruption. Today, Porac continues to progress, supported by infrastructure developments and its strategic location, making it a growing hub in Central Luzon.

Puning Hot Springs

Puning Hot Springs is a natural hot spring resort located in Porac, Pampanga, near the slopes of Mount Pinatubo. The resort features hot and warm pools fed by natural mineral-rich waters, surrounded by lush greenery, making it a relaxing getaway for visitors. It’s a popular spot for swimming, soaking, and therapeutic relaxation, especially after trekking Mount Pinatubo. The area also offers scenic views of the countryside and nearby rivers, giving guests a peaceful and rejuvenating experience.

Miyamit Falls

Miyamit Falls is a hidden waterfall and nature spot located in the mountainous areas of Porac, Pampanga. The falls feature crystal-clear water cascading into natural pools, surrounded by lush forests and tropical vegetation. It is popular for hiking, trekking, swimming, and picnics, making it a favorite destination for nature lovers and adventurers. Access usually involves a short trek through forest trails, adding a sense of adventure to the visit.

SandBox Pampanga

The Sandbox Pampanga is an adventure park located in Porac, Pampanga that offers a wide range of outdoor activities and attractions for thrill-seekers and families. It features obstacle courses, giant swings, ATV rides, and various team-building facilities set in a spacious natural environment. With its combination of adventure and open landscapes, Sandbox Pampanga provides an exciting destination for recreation, bonding, and outdoor experiences.

Babo Robot

The Babo Robot is a tourist attraction in Porac, Pampanga known for its giant robot structure made from recycled materials. It is a 30-foot fighting machine from the 1996 film Robo Warriors, which was filmed mostly in Porac Town. It features a creative and artistic installation that showcases local craftsmanship and imagination. With its unique design and photo-worthy appeal, Babo Robot serves as a distinctive landmark that attracts visitors interested in art, culture, and visual attractions.
