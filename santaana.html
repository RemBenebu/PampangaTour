SANTA ANA

Santa Ana, formerly known as “Balen Pinpin,” is a 3rd class agricultural municipality covering about 39.84 sq km, known for producing tilapia, corn, and palay. It has 14 barangays with a population of 61,537 as of 2020. The town is recognized for its annual Majiganggas Festival and the historic St. Anne Parish Church established in 1853, reflecting its rich cultural and religious heritage.

Santa Ana Night Market

The Santa Ana Night Market in Pampanga, launched in mid-2025, is a vibrant new food hub located in San Pedro, offering a laid-back, community-focused street food scene. It operates as a bustling evening destination with diverse food stalls, street food, and sometimes live entertainment.

Santa Ana Municipal Grounds

The Santa Ana Municipal Grounds is a well designed and vibrant public space that serves as a central hub for community activities and local events. It features a colorful and organized layout that enhances its inviting atmosphere, making it a pleasant place for gatherings, celebrations, and municipal programs.

Santa Ana Parish Church

The Santa Ana Parish Church, also known as the St. Anne Parish Church, is a historic Spanish era Roman Catholic church established in 1853. Known for its classic architectural design and prominent bell tower, it stands as a significant religious and cultural landmark in the town, reflecting both its heritage and enduring faith of the community

San Agustin Nature's Picnic Park

The San Agustin Nature’s Picnic Park in Santa Ana is a relaxing outdoor destination surrounded by greenery, offering a peaceful setting for family outings and leisure activities. It features open spaces for picnics, shaded areas, and a calm natural environment, making it an ideal spot for visitors who want to unwind and enjoy nature.
