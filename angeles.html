ANGELES CITY

Angeles City is a bustling, highly urbanized city in Pampanga, Central Luzon, Philippines, known as a major commercial, entertainment, and historic hub. Located about 88 km north of Manila, it is famous for its vibrant nightlife in the Balibago district, casinos, Clark International Airport, and proximity to Mt. Pinatubo.

Aqua Planet

Aqua Planet is one of the largest and most modern water parks in the Philippines, located inside Clark Freeport Zone. It features over 38 water slides and attractions spread across a 10-hectare area, offering exciting rides like wave pools, lazy rivers, and high-speed slides for all ages. The park is known for its world-class facilities, safety standards, and cashless system, along with multiple dining areas and relaxing cabanas—making it perfect for families, friends, and school outings.

Pisamban Maragul

The Minor Basilica and Parish of Our Lady of the Most Holy Rosary, also known as Pisamban Maragul, was originally built in 1877 and is one of the oldest churches in Angeles City. Known for its beautiful architecture and serene atmosphere, it reflects the rich cultural and religious heritage of the area. As the first minor basilica in Pampanga, it continues to stand as a symbol of faith, history, and timeless charm.

Dinosaurs Island

Dinosaurs Island is a fun and educational theme park where visitors can experience life-sized moving dinosaurs in a jungle-like setting. It features animatronic dinosaurs that move and make sounds, making it feel like you’re in the prehistoric era. The park also includes interactive attractions like a fossil museum, jungle safari, and 7D rides, making it perfect for families and students.

Clark Parade Grounds

The Clark Parade Grounds is a spacious open park located within the Clark Freeport Zone in Pampanga. It is known for its wide green fields, tree-lined paths, and relaxed environment, making it suitable for walking, jogging, biking, and outdoor activities. The area also reflects a mix of historical significance and modern recreation, serving as a peaceful space where visitors can enjoy nature and spend time with family and friends.
