GUAGUA

Guagua is a first-class municipality in Pampanga, located in the central part of the province. Its name is believed to have originated from the Kapampangan word “wawa”, meaning river mouth, as it lies near the Guagua River. Guagua is known for its rich history, colonial-era churches, and cultural heritage. Landmarks include St. Augustine Parish Church, heritage houses, and local plazas. The town is also famous for local products and festivals, reflecting the vibrant Kapampangan culture. Economically, Guagua serves as a commercial hub with markets, schools, and small businesses supporting both locals and neighboring towns.

Santiago Apostol Parish Church (Betis Church)

The Santiago Apostol Parish Church (Betis Church) is a historic Roman Catholic church in Guagua, Pampanga known for its remarkable Baroque architecture and intricate ceiling paintings. Often referred to as the “Sistine Chapel of the Philippines,” it showcases detailed artwork, wood carvings, and religious heritage that reflect the rich cultural and artistic traditions of the region.

La Consolacion Parish Church

The La Consolacion Parish Church is a Roman Catholic church in Guagua, Pampanga known for its religious significance and community role. It serves as a place of worship for locals and reflects the town’s strong Catholic traditions. With its simple yet meaningful structure, the church stands as a center for faith, gatherings, and spiritual life in the community.

Plaza Burgos

The Plaza Burgos, located in the town proper (barangay) of Guagua, Pampanga, is a historic public park and central gathering place renovated in 2014. Also known as Manuel P. Santiago Park, it features monuments that honor local history and serves as a bustling hub beside the public market, acting as a key landmark in the center of the town.

Fabrika Dining Villas & Fabrika Villas

Fabrika Dining & Villas is a charming dining and staycation destination in Guagua that blends good food, relaxing ambiance, and unique accommodation all in one place. A restaurant with indoor and outdoor seating serving Filipino and Asian dishes, including Kapampangan favorites like kare‑kare, okoy, sisig, and more. The setting is relaxed, surrounded by greenery and a pond — great for meals with friends or family. Visitors say the place has a rustic, aesthetic vibe, making it great for dining, relaxing, and taking photos. It’s also becoming a popular spot for occasions, romantic dinners, and weekend breaks away from the city bustle.
