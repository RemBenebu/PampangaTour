MABALACAT CITY

Mabalacat City is a component city in Pampanga, Philippines, that derives its name from the Kapampangan phrase meaning “full of balacats” or “forest of balacats.” It refers to the abundant Ziziphus talanai (balacat) trees, a fourth-class timber native to the area. Founded in 1712, the city is known for its rich cultural heritage and its proximity to the Clark Freeport Zone.

Kamikaze East Airfield

Kamikaze East Airfield is a historical World War II site in Mabalacat City, Pampanga. It is known as the place where Japanese kamikaze (suicide) pilots first launched their missions in the Philippines during October 1944. Today, the original airfield no longer exists, but a peace memorial and monument stands on the site to honor history and promote peace—not to glorify war.

Clark Safari and Adventure Park

Clark Safari and Adventure Park is a large wildlife and adventure park located inside Clark Freeport Zone in Mabalacat City. It covers around 40 hectares and is home to over 1,500 animals from about 70 species, including lions, tigers, bears, monkeys, and more. Visitors can enjoy a safari-style experience, where they can walk through wide open areas, see animals up close, feed some of them, and even ride safari vehicles. The park also offers fun activities like ATV rides and interactive attractions for all ages.

Zoocobia Fun Zoo

The Zoocobia Fun Zoo is a family-friendly attraction located in Clark Freeport Zone, Mabalacat City, Pampanga that offers a fun and educational experience centered on animals and nature. It features a variety of animal exhibits, interactive activities, and adventure-based attractions that allow visitors to observe and engage with different species. With its combination of recreation and learning, Zoocobia provides an enjoyable environment for visitors of all ages.

Green Asia Leisure ATV

Green Asia Leisure ATV 4X4 Off-Road is an exciting off-road adventure attraction where visitors can ride all-terrain vehicles (ATVs) through rugged trails, rivers, and jungle-like paths. Located near the Sacobia River area, it offers guided ATV tours that take you across rocky terrains, lahar fields, and forest trails, giving a thrilling outdoor experience. The activity is perfect for adventure seekers, as you can drive your own ATV while exploring nature with the help of trained guides.
