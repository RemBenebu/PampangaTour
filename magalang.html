MAGALANG

Magalang is a first-class municipality in Pampanga, located at the foot of Mount Arayat. It is known for its agricultural lands, rich cultural heritage, and scenic landscapes. The town has a mix of historical landmarks, churches, and festivals, reflecting the Kapampangan culture and traditions. Magalang is also recognized for horticulture, rice and vegetable production, making it an important agricultural hub in the province.

San Bartolome Parish Church

The San Bartolome Parish Church is a historic Catholic church located in Magalang, Pampanga, dedicated to Saint Bartholomew the Apostle. Built during the Spanish colonial period, the church features colonial architecture with a simple yet elegant façade, bell tower, and traditional altar, reflecting the architectural style of that era. It serves as an important religious and cultural center for the local community, hosting fiestas, masses, and other parish activities that showcase the devotion of the people of Magalang.

Torre Heliografico

BThe Torre Heliografico is a historical Spanish-era communication tower built in 1863 in Magalang, Pampanga. Erected by Spanish general Ricardo Monet, it used heliograph technology, small mirrors mounted on a tripod, to send Morse Code signals, primarily to aid in military operations against revolutionary forces.

Pampanga State Agricultural University (Magalang, Pampanga)

The Pampanga State Agricultural University, is a higher education institution in Magalang, Pampanga, specializing in agriculture, horticulture, and related sciences. The college provides academic programs, research facilities, and training grounds for students pursuing careers in agriculture, animal husbandry, and agribusiness, helping support the province’s agricultural development. PSAU also hosts community outreach programs, agricultural demonstrations, and training workshops, making it a key center for education, research, and community development in Pampanga.

Magalang Town Plaza and Old Municipal Hall

The Magalang Town Plaza and Old Municipal Hall is a historical landmark in Magalang, Pampanga that serves as a central public space and a reminder of the town’s heritage. The plaza provides an open area for community gatherings and local events, while the old municipal hall reflects the town’s colonial-era architecture and administrative history. Together, they stand as important symbols of Magalang’s cultural identity and civic life.
