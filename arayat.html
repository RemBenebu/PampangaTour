ARAYAT

Arayat is a landlocked, agricultural municipality in Pampanga, Philippines, located 22 km from San Fernando and 88 km from Manila. Renowned for the majestic, dormant volcanic Mount Arayat, it serves as a center for agriculture (rice/sugarcane) and ecotourism, featuring 30 barangays and a population of 144,875 as of 2020.

Mount Arayat

Mount Arayat is an extinct volcano and the most iconic natural landmark of Pampanga, rising about 1,026 meters above sea level in Arayat town. It is part of Mount Arayat National Park, a protected area that features lush forests, hiking trails, rivers, and picnic spots. The mountain is also rich in local folklore, including tales of the Kapampangan goddess Mariang Sinukuan. Hiking Mount Arayat is popular among adventure seekers and nature lovers, offering panoramic views of Pampanga and nearby provinces.

Gintung Pakpak Eco Park

The Gintung Pakpak Eco Park is a nature and eco-tourism destination located in Arayat, Pampanga that offers a peaceful environment surrounded by greenery and views of Mount Arayat. It features outdoor activities, scenic spots, and spaces for relaxation, making it a suitable place for nature appreciation and leisure. With its natural setting and refreshing atmosphere, it provides visitors with an opportunity to unwind while enjoying the beauty of the outdoors.

Bale Batu

Bale Batu is a unique man-made stone structure and tourist attraction in Arayat, Pampanga. It is designed to resemble Cambodia’s Angkor Wat, featuring large stone walls, arches, and sculptures. The site is surrounded by gardens and landscaped pathways, making it a popular spot for photography, leisurely walks, and cultural appreciation. Bale Batu also serves as a symbol of creativity and local craftsmanship, blending architecture with the natural environment of Arayat.

Tree House Bike Park

The Tree House Bike Park is a well-known biking destination in Arayat, Pampanga that offers outdoor trails and open spaces for cycling enthusiasts. Surrounded by natural scenery, it provides a relaxing environment where visitors can enjoy biking, leisure activities, and time in nature.
