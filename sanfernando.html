CITY OF SAN FERNANDO

The City of San Fernando is the capital city of Pampanga, known as a center of commerce, culture, and government in the province. It is famous for its vibrant traditions, including the annual Giant Lantern Festival, which showcases the creativity and craftsmanship of local communities. With its blend of historical landmarks, urban development, and cultural heritage, the city serves as an important hub in Central Luzon.

The Orchid Gardens

The Orchid Gardens is a well-known resort and leisure complex in the heart of San Fernando City. It covers around 2.5 hectares and offers a mix of hotel rooms, villas, restaurants, event venues, and a swimming pool, making it ideal for both relaxation and special occasions like weddings and parties. The place is often called the “Jewel of San Fernando” because of its elegant ambiance, landscaped gardens, and complete facilities for guests and visitors.

Metropolitan Cathedral of Saint Ferdinand

The Metropolitan Cathedral of Saint Ferdinand is a historic Roman Catholic church and the main seat of the Archdiocese of San Fernando. Originally built in 1754 by Augustinian friars, the cathedral has gone through several reconstructions due to fires and war, with the current structure completed in 1948 in a neoclassical architectural style. It is dedicated to Saint Ferdinand III of Castile and is known for its large dome, elegant façade, and important role in Pampanga’s religious and historical life.

SkyRanch Pampanga

The Sky Ranch Pampanga is an amusement park located in the City of San Fernando, Pampanga that offers a variety of rides and attractions for visitors of all ages. It features the Pampanga Eye, the tallest Ferris wheel in the Philippines at about 65 meters high, providing panoramic views of the surrounding area. With its mix of thrill rides, family-friendly attractions, and scenic experience, the park serves as a recreational destination for leisure and entertainment.

Capital Town Rainwater Park

The Capital Town Rainwater Park is a modern eco-friendly park and tourist attraction located inside Capital Town in San Fernando, Pampanga. It is a 2-hectare landscaped park designed not only for recreation but also as a flood-control system that collects and stores rainwater. The park features a man-made lagoon with fountains, gardens, jogging paths, playgrounds, a mini football field, amphitheater, and viewing deck, making it a perfect place for relaxation and outdoor activities.
