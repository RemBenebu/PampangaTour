SAN SIMON

San Simon is a municipality in Pampanga known for its growing industrial and commercial activity. It is strategically located along major highways, making it accessible and suitable for trade and business development. Despite its modernization, San Simon maintains its local community traditions and reflects a balance between urban growth and provincial life.

San Simon Parish Church (St. Peter and Paul Parish)

The San Simon Parish Church, also known as the St. Peter and Paul Parish Church, is a historic Catholic church located in San Simon, Pampanga. Built during the Spanish colonial period, it features classic colonial architecture, including a bell tower, stone façade, and intricately designed altar, reflecting the style of that era. The church serves as the spiritual and cultural center of San Simon, hosting regular masses, fiestas, and parish activities, and remains an important landmark of the town’s religious heritage.

San Simon Town Plaza

The San Simon Town Plaza is the central gathering place of the municipality, located near the town hall and church. It serves as a hub for community activities, such as festivals, public events, and social gatherings. The plaza is also a relaxing spot for locals and visitors, featuring open spaces, benches, and landscaped areas.

Local Farms and Agri-Tourism Spots

Local Farms and Agri-Tourism Spots in San Simon, Pampanga showcase the town’s agricultural heritage and offer visitors a chance to experience rural life and farming activities. These farms feature rice paddies, vegetable gardens, and small livestock areas, and often welcome visitors for farm tours, harvesting experiences, and educational activities. Agri-tourism in San Simon highlights the importance of agriculture to the local economy while giving tourists a hands-on experience of Kapampangan farming traditions.

Barangay Cultural Sites and Festivals

Barangay Cultural Sites and Festivals in San Simon, Pampanga celebrate the rich Kapampangan heritage, traditions, and community spirit. Each barangay often hosts festivals, town fiestas, and cultural events, featuring folk dances, music, local crafts, and traditional cuisine. These activities not only strengthen community bonds but also attract visitors who want to experience local culture.
