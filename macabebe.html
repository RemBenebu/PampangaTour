MACABEBE

The Macabebe is a municipality in Pampanga known for its rich history, river-based lifestyle, and strong fishing industry. Located along the Pampanga River and near Manila Bay, it has long been an important center for trade and livelihood. With its cultural heritage and connection to waterways, Macabebe reflects the traditional way of life in coastal Pampanga while continuing to develop as a community.

San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish

The San Nicolas de Tolentino Parish Church is a historic Catholic church located in Macabebe, Pampanga, dedicated to Saint Nicholas of Tolentino. Established during the Spanish colonial period, the church showcases classic colonial architecture, including a sturdy façade, bell tower, and traditional interiors. It has long served as a center of faith and community life, hosting religious celebrations, fiestas, and parish events that reflect the deep devotion of the locals.

Barangay Consuelo Mangrove Site

Barangay Consuelo Mangrove Site is an eco-tourism and conservation area in Macabebe, Pampanga, dedicated to preserving mangrove forests along the riverbanks. The site features lush mangrove trees, wooden boardwalks, and educational signage, making it ideal for nature walks, birdwatching, and environmental learning. It also serves as a community-led reforestation and awareness project, helping protect the coastal ecosystem while providing locals and visitors a peaceful natural retreat.

Macabebe River Tours

The Macabebe River Tours offers a scenic and immersive experience along the waterways of Macabebe, Pampanga. Visitors can explore the town’s river-based lifestyle while enjoying views of mangroves, fishing communities, and the natural surroundings. With its relaxing atmosphere and cultural charm, it provides a unique way to discover the heritage and beauty of this riverside town.

Cepella de San Gabriel

The Capella de San Gabriel (San Gabriel Chapel) is a local chapel located in Caduang Tete, Macabebe, Pampanga. Situated along the Apalit-Macabebe-Masantol Road near the Barangay Hall and Elementary School, it serves as a center for worship in the area and is often associated with the Holy Family Parish.
