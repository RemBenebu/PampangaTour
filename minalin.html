MINALIN

Minalin is a first-class municipality in Pampanga, located along the banks of the Pampanga River. It is known for its rich history, vibrant culture, and colorful traditions. The town is famous for the “Aguman Sanduk” or “Minalin Festival”, showcasing Kapampangan heritage, folk traditions, and local crafts. Minalin also has historic churches, ancestral houses, and community landmarks reflecting its colonial past. Economically, the town thrives on agriculture, small-scale industries, and local markets, while maintaining its cultural identity and traditions.

Sta. Monica Parish Church

The Sta. Monica Parish Church, commonly known as Minalin Church, is a Baroque Roman Catholic church in Minalin, Pampanga that was built during the Spanish era. It was declared a National Cultural Treasure on August 27, 2011 by the National Commission for Culture and the Arts and the National Museum of the Philippines, highlighting its historical and cultural significance as a heritage landmark.

Minalin Sunset Park

The Minalin Sunset Park is a scenic public park in Minalin, Pampanga known for its relaxing atmosphere and picturesque sunset views. It features open spaces for walking and leisure, along with numerous food stalls that offer a variety of local snacks and refreshments, making it a pleasant spot for both relaxation and casual dining.

The 1619 Mural

The 1619 Mural in Minalin, Pampanga is a historic artwork discovered in the local parish church, believed to date back to 1619 during the Spanish colonial period. This mural depicts religious and cultural themes, offering a glimpse into the artistic style, beliefs, and daily life of early Kapampangans. It is considered one of the oldest known murals in the province, highlighting Minalin’s rich cultural and historical heritage. The mural is an important cultural landmark, preserved for both locals and visitors as a testament to the town’s artistic and religious history.

Minalin Museum and Library

The Minalin Museum and Library is a local cultural and educational facility in Minalin, Pampanga that showcases the town’s history, heritage, and traditions. It houses historical artifacts, documents, and exhibits that help preserve and promote the identity of the community, while also serving as a space for learning and public access to information.
