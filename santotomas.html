SANTO TOMAS

Santo Tomas is a municipality in Pampanga known for its rich agricultural lands, cultural traditions, and historic landmarks. The town features heritage churches, ancestral houses, and local festivals, reflecting the Kapampangan culture and history. Agriculture, particularly rice and vegetable farming, is a major part of the town’s economy.

Pampanga Pottery and Agritourism Park

The Pampanga Pottery and Agritourism Park in Santo Tomas is a recreational and educational destination that combines traditional pottery-making and agricultural experiences. Visitors can try hands-on pottery workshops, see clay crafting demonstrations, and explore the farm areas where local crops are grown. The park highlights Kapampangan craftsmanship and agricultural practices, providing both entertainment and learning opportunities.

St. John the Baptist Parish Church

The St. John the Baptist Parish Church is a historic Catholic church located in Santo Tomas, Pampanga, dedicated to Saint John the Baptist. Built during the Spanish colonial period, the church features colonial architecture with a bell tower, stone façade, and traditional altar, reflecting the religious and cultural heritage of the town. It serves as a spiritual hub for the local community, hosting regular masses, town fiestas, and parish activities that highlight Santo Tomas’ faith and traditions.

Santo Tomas Town Plaza

The Santo Tomas Town Plaza is the central hub of the municipality, located near the church and municipal hall. It serves as a gathering place for community events, local festivals, and social activities. The plaza features open spaces, landscaped areas, and seating, making it a relaxing spot for both residents and visitors

Rancho at Mula de Victoria

Rancho at Mula de Victoria is a Christmas-themed destination in Santo Tomas, Pampanga, offering a Disney-inspired magical experience with dazzling lights, a castle mapping show, and artificial snow in a "Winter Village". Located in Barangay Sto. Niño, this affordable attraction features festive music, a, food bazaar, and nightly fireworks.
