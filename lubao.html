LUBAO

The Lubao, known as the “Cradle of Kapampangan Civilization,” is one of the oldest settlements in the country. It is famous for the 16th-century St. Augustine Parish Church, its distinction as the birthplace of two Philippine presidents (Diosdado Macapagal and Gloria Macapagal-Arroyo), the Sampaguita Festival, and its strong agricultural production of rice, sugarcane, and fish.

San Agustin Parish Church

The San Agustin Parish Church is a historic Roman Catholic church located in Lubao, Pampanga, dedicated to Saint Augustine of Hippo. Built during the Spanish colonial period, the church features Spanish-era architecture with a bell tower, elegant façade, and a traditional altar. It serves as an important religious, cultural, and historical landmark for the Lubao community. The church hosts fiestas, masses, and local celebrations, reflecting the town’s strong religious and cultural traditions.

Museo at Aklatan ni Diosdado Macapagal

The Museo at Aklatan ni Diosdado Macapagal is a museum and library in Lubao, Pampanga dedicated to the life and legacy of former Philippine President Diosdado Macapagal. It houses personal artifacts, memorabilia, photographs, and historical documents that highlight his contributions to the country. Serving as both an educational and cultural site, it provides visitors with insight into Philippine history and the achievements of one of the nation’s notable leaders.

Museo ni Jose B. Lingad

Museo ni Jose B. Lingad is a biographical and historical museum located in Lubao, Pampanga, dedicated to honoring the life and legacy of Jose B. Lingad, a distinguished Filipino lawyer, World War II veteran, and public servant from Pampanga. The museum is housed in his ancestral home in Barangay San Nicolas 1st. Inside, it features personal belongings, memorabilia, photos, and exhibits that tell the story of Lingad’s journey from his youth in Lubao to his significant roles in government. It was established to educate visitors about Lingad’s life and inspiring legacy.

Lubao Bamboo Hub & Eco-Park

The Lubao Bamboo Hub and Eco-Park is an eco-tourism destination located along the riverbank of Sta. Catalina in Lubao, Pampanga that promotes bamboo as a sustainable resource and helps protect against soil erosion. Managed by the local government, it features bamboo structures, scenic pathways, and activities such as biking and kayaking, offering a relaxing nature experience for visitors.
