FLORIDABLANCA

Floridablanca is a municipality in Pampanga known for its rich agricultural lands and natural surroundings. It is home to various local industries and eco-tourism spots, reflecting a balance between rural livelihood and gradual development. With its scenic landscapes and cultural heritage, Floridablanca offers a glimpse of traditional provincial life in Central Luzon.

Nabuclod Mountain Views

Nabuclod Mountain Views is a scenic highland viewpoint and hiking destination located in Floridablanca, Pampanga. Situated near the mountainous areas close to Mount Pinatubo, it offers breathtaking panoramic views of mountains, clouds, and surrounding landscapes, especially during sunrise and sunset. The area is popular for hiking, camping, and photography, with cool weather and peaceful surroundings that make it perfect for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

Saint Joseph the Worker Parish Church

The Saint Joseph the Worker Parish Church is a Catholic church located in Floridablanca, Pampanga, dedicated to Saint Joseph, the patron saint of workers. The church serves as an important religious center for the local community, where masses, fiestas, and other parish activities are held. It reflects the strong faith and traditions of the Kapampangan people. Known for its simple yet welcoming design, the church provides a peaceful place for prayer and reflection for both locals and visitors.

Palakol River

Palakol River is a natural river attraction in Floridablanca, Pampanga, known for its clear flowing water and rocky riverbed. Formed by the lahar flows from Mount Pinatubo, the river features large volcanic rocks, cool water, and scenic surroundings, making it a refreshing spot for visitors. It is popular for river trekking, swimming, picnics, and camping, especially for those looking for a quiet and nature-filled getaway.

Bale Kastila

The Bale Kastila (Spanish House) is recognized as the oldest house in the town of Floridablanca, Pampanga and stands as a monument to history. This 19th-century architectural structure still remains today, offering a glimpse into its historical significance amidst the eerie narratives associated with it.
