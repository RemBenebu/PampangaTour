CANDABA

The Candaba is a municipality in Pampanga known for its vast wetlands and fertile agricultural lands. It is widely recognized as a prime destination for bird-watching, as thousands of migratory birds visit its marshlands each year. With its rich natural landscapes, peaceful rural atmosphere, and ecological significance, Candaba offers a unique experience that highlights both the beauty of nature and the importance of environmental conservation.

Bird sanctuary

The Candaba Bird Sanctuary is a famous wetland and wildlife conservation area located in Candaba, Pampanga. It serves as a temporary home for thousands of migratory birds from countries like Siberia, Japan, and China during the dry season (November to May). The sanctuary features swamps, rice fields, and ponds, which provide ideal habitats for species such as herons, egrets, kingfishers, and the Philippine duck.Birdwatching tours, photography, and eco-tourism activities are popular here, making it a must-visit spot for nature lovers and wildlife enthusiasts.

Pinak Food Park

The Pinak Food Park is a food destination located in Candaba, Pampanga that is known as one of the longest food parks in the area. It features a variety of food stalls offering different local dishes and street food in a lively and casual setting. With its wide selection of food options and relaxed atmosphere, it provides a place where visitors can enjoy dining while experiencing the local flavors of the community.

St. Andres Apostol Parish

St. Andres Apostol Parish Church is a historic Roman Catholic church located in Candaba, Pampanga. The church is dedicated to Saint Andrew the Apostle and is known for its Spanish-era architecture, featuring a simple but elegant façade, bell tower, and traditional altar. It serves as an important religious and cultural center for the local community, hosting masses, fiestas, and other parish activities.

Lola Corazon Leisure Farm

The Lola Corazon Leisure Farm is a leisure and farm destination located in Candaba, Pampanga that offers a relaxing countryside experience surrounded by nature. It features farm activities, open spaces, and accommodations designed for rest, recreation, and family bonding. With its peaceful environment and agricultural setting, it provides visitors with a refreshing escape while enjoying the charm of rural life.
