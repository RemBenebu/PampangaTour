SAN LUIS

San Luis is a municipality in Pampanga located in the central part of the province. It is known for its agricultural lands, traditional Kapampangan culture, and historic landmarks. The town has a mix of heritage churches, local festivals, and ancestral houses, reflecting the rich history and traditions of its people. Agriculture, particularly rice and sugarcane farming, plays a key role in the town’s economy.

San Luis Gonzaga Parish Church

The San Luis Gonzaga Parish Church is a historic Catholic church located in San Luis, Pampanga, dedicated to Saint Aloysius Gonzaga (San Luis Gonzaga). Built during the Spanish colonial period, the church features colonial architecture with a simple façade, bell tower, and traditional altar, reflecting the design style of that era. It serves as a spiritual and cultural center for the local community, hosting masses, fiestas, and parish events that highlight the town’s devotion and traditions.

San Luis Park

The San Luis Park is a public park in San Luis, Pampanga that provides a relaxing open space for residents and visitors. It features areas for walking, resting, and community activities, offering a simple and peaceful environment where people can enjoy leisure time and outdoor gatherings.

Hukbalahap WWII Monument

The Hukbalahap WWII Monument in San Luis, Pampanga is a historical landmark commemorating the Hukbalahap (Hukbong Bayan Laban sa Hapon) guerrilla fighters who resisted the Japanese occupation during World War II. The monument honors the bravery and sacrifices of local heroes, serving as a reminder of Pampanga’s role in the Philippine resistance movement. It is often visited for historical reflection and educational purposes.

Idol TikTok Farm Resort

Lot-Gar Farm Resort, popularly known as the "Idol TikTok Farm," is a scenic agritourism destination located in Brgy. Santa Catalina, San Luis, Pampanga. Owned by social media personality Romeo Catacutan (famously known as "Idol TikTok"), the farm is celebrated for its advocacy of generosity and "Good Morning" positivity.
